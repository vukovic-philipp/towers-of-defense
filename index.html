Towers of Defense

Hundreds of enemies pour through the canyon toward your gate. Build on the rocks and burn, shoot and blast them down.

How to play
  • Tap a rock to pick a spot, then choose a tower. Towers only fit on rocks.
  • Gun: quick single shots. Laser: a beam that pierces everything on its line.
  • Flame: sets a cone on fire. Mortar: lobs shells that explode on the thickest clump.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade it. Each tower has its own upgrade tree, and every choice locks out its alternatives, so pick a role. Selling returns 70 percent of what you spent.
  • Enemies that reach the gate on the right cost lives. Big ones cost more.
  • Waves arrive on a timer, so build before the countdown ends.
  • New enemy types show up as waves go on: some armored, some shielded, some heal, some flicker out of reach.
  • When a run ends you earn research points. Spend them in the research tree for permanent bonuses.
  • The workshop lets you design your own turret from a base behaviour, stat points and perks.

Needs a browser with WebGPU: Safari on iPadOS 26, or current Chrome and Edge.
