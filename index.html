⚔️ Towers of Defense

Goblin balls tumble down a Voronoi cave.
Waves never stop — you only lose.

How to play
  • Tap anywhere in the cave to place a tower — no grid. Towers never block balls.
  • Cannons blast balls around, archers nudge them, Frost slows them. Balls have real mass and collide.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade, sell or change its targeting.
  • Balls that reach the castle at the bottom cost lives (bosses cost 10). Every tower you own makes the next one 7% pricier.
  • When you fall, the waves you survived earn shards to spend on permanent research.
