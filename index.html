Towers of Defense

Balls tumble through a procedurally generated cave toward your castle. Hold the line as long as you can.

How to play
  • Tap anywhere in the cave to place a tower. There is no grid, and towers never block the balls.
  • Archers nudge balls, Cannons blast them back, Frost slows them. Heavy balls move less.
  • Balls that reach the castle on the right cost lives. Bosses cost 10.
  • Every tower you own makes the next one 7% pricier.
  • When you fall, the waves you survived earn shards for permanent research.
