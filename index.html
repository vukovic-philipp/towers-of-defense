⚔️ Towers of Defense

Hold the road against an endless goblin horde.
Waves never stop — you only lose.

How to play
  • Tap an empty grass tile, then pick a tower.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade, sell or change its targeting.
  • Goblins that reach the castle cost lives. Bosses cost 10.
  • When you fall, the waves you survived earn shards to spend on permanent research.
